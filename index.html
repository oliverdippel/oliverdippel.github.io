About

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Liverpool. My work focuses on reinforcement learning under uncertainty and foundation-model–driven agents.

I’m interested in combining transformers with sequential decision making to build agents that can plan, adapt, and generalize beyond their training distribution.

Research

Core themes: belief-aware policies, uncertainty-aware optimization, and feedback-driven post-training. I care about reliable agent behavior: stability, failure modes, and robust evaluation.

Long-term goal: agents that can learn continually, reason over long horizons, and adapt safely in open-ended settings.
