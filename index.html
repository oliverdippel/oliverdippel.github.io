About

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Liverpool. My work focuses on reinforcement learning under uncertainty and foundation-model–driven agents.

I’m interested in combining transformers with sequential decision making to build agents that can plan, adapt, and generalize beyond their training distribution.

During my Ph.D., I briefly worked at ZeroAI on safety/robustness evaluation for autonomous driving systems. Prior to that, I interned at Novartis building internal tools for scientific workflows.

Research interests

Decision making under uncertainty. Belief-aware policies, posterior regularization, and robust exploration–exploitation trade-offs.

Foundation-model agents. Transformer policy priors, in-context adaptation, long-horizon planning and memory.

Learning from feedback. Reward design, noisy/underspecified feedback, RLHF-style objectives, and failure analysis.

Evaluation. Stress-testing, distribution shift, and reproducible benchmarks for agent robustness.
